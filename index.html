Antimony-Free Flame Retardant Solution for PVC Compounds

  • 🔹 Replaces 50%–100% of Sb₂O₃
  • 🔹 Saves NTD 7–9/kg (50% sub) or NTD 16–17/kg (Sb-free)

  • 🔹 Maintains Shore A hardness & processability via TCP tuning

Antimony trioxide (Sb₂O₃) is restricted or phased out across toys (EN 71-3), food contact materials, eco-labels (EU Ecolabel / TCO), and major electronics brands (Apple, Dell, HP). Adopting UTFR-NAL ensures compliance while cutting material costs.
